# Edge Log Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11/20/2026

**Edge Log does not collect any data.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive anything you enter in the app.

## Where your information lives
- **On your device.** Your knives, sharpening sessions, notes, photos and settings are stored on your iPhone.
- **In your own iCloud.** If you are signed in to iCloud, Edge Log syncs your data through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) in your private iCloud storage. Only you can access it, and we cannot see it.
- **With people you invite.** If you choose to share your household, Apple's iCloud sharing gives the people you invite access to your knives, either read-write or view-only. You control who is invited and can stop sharing at any time. Apple handles this; the data never passes through any server of ours.

## Features that touch other services
- **Apple Intelligence.** The optional "fill in details" feature runs on your device using Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any other server.
- **Search the Web.** This opens a Google search in an in-app Safari view. Google's own privacy policy applies to that page. Edge Log does not send Google anything beyond the search text you see.
- **Notifications.** Sharpening reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push messages are sent from any server of ours.
- **Camera and photos.** If you take or add a photo of a knife, it stays on your device (and in your iCloud, as above).

## Export and import
Backups you export (JSON or CSV) are saved wherever you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. We never see them.

## Children
Edge Log collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
